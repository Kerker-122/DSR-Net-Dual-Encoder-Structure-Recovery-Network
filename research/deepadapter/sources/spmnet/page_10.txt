3.4 Hybrid Shape-Aware Loss

Since PSD introduces shape-guided decoding based on the CCS prior, the final prediction should
not only maintain pixel-level regional accuracy but also preserve geometric consistency with the
target structure. However, conventional segmentation losses mainly focus on classification errors
and provide limited constraints on boundary directions and structural continuity, especially for
camouflaged  objects.  Therefore, we propose  the HSAL, which  jointly  considers  regional
supervision and shape consistency to optimize the final prediction. HSAL is formulated as:
