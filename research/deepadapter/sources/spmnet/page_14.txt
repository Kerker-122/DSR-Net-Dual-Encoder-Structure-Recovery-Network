strategy. By combining SAG and ICCS, PSD enhances the reliability of decoding features and
preserves the  structural information of camouflaged  targets.  Specifically, SAG reduces the
interference from camouflage backgrounds during cross-level feature fusion, providing more
reliable representations for subsequent refinement. Then, ICCS introduces explicit geometric
constraints through the CCS shape prior and progressively corrects the predictions through iterative
refinement, which improves the recovery of ambiguous boundaries and slender structures. In
addition, HSAL further constrains the consistency between predicted regions and target geometry,
allowing SPMNet to obtain more accurate segmentation results.

b.  Computational Complexity Analysis

Besides segmentation performance, we further evaluate the computational complexity of different
methods in terms of parameters, GFLOPs, and FPS. To ensure reliable FPS measurement, all
experiments are conducted on a single NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU. Before testing, 20 warm-up
iterations are performed to reduce the influence of GPU initialization latency, and the final FPS is
calculated based on the average inference time of 200 consecutive runs.

As shown in Table 3, SPMNet achieves a favorable balance between segmentation accuracy and
computational cost. Compared with several methods based on large vision encoders, SPMNet
avoids excessive parameter growth and computational complexity. Although introducing the shape-
prior guided progressive decoding strategy and ICCS refinement mechanism, SPMNet maintains
reasonable model efficiency, demonstrating its effectiveness in balancing segmentation performance
and computational overhead.

4.4.2 Qualitative Comparisons

Fig. 4 presents qualitative comparisons of different methods on representative camouflaged marine
animal samples. It can be observed that most methods achieve similar segmentation results when
the target regions are clear and background interference is limited. However, when facing complex
background interference, low-contrast targets, and marine organisms with complex structures,
existing  methods  often  suffer from  blurred  boundaries,  incomplete  structures,  and  false
